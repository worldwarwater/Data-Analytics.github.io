Marketing Campaign Segmentation & ROI

Python · SQL · Scikit-learn · SciPy · Looker Studio

Segmented 2,205 retail customers (2,240 before cleaning) with RFM scoring and K-Means clustering, compared campaign response across segments and channels, and checked the differences with 10 statistical tests.

  • Hibernating customers are 68% of the base and 61% of spend. Loyal customers are 10% of the base but 21% of spend.
  • Champions respond to campaigns at 48%, versus 11% for Hibernating customers.
  • Income explains about 68% of the variance in total spend (Pearson r = 0.82).

Channel ROI in the notebooks uses assumed contact costs ($5 web, $15 catalog, $3 store), since the dataset has no cost data.

Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Looker Studio

Explored churn across 7,043 telecom customers and trained a logistic regression model (ROC-AUC about 0.74) that scores each customer's risk of leaving.

  • Contract type is the strongest driver: month-to-month customers churn at 42%, against 17% on one-year and 9% on two-year contracts.
  • Customers in their first two years churn at roughly 36–37%, versus about 21–23% after that.
  • Churned customers pay more per month ($69 vs $64), and fiber customers churn most of the internet types.

0 fewer churned customers, about $0 a month in retained revenue.

Assumes converted customers take on the one-year churn rate (17.2%) and keep the month-to-month average bill ($65.69).

Job Market Analysis Pipeline

Python · SQL · SQLite · SciPy · Looker Studio

An end-to-end pipeline: generated 2,500 data-role job postings, loaded them into a normalized SQLite database, answered 10 business questions in SQL and tested the results with t-tests, ANOVA and chi-square.

  • ML tools (TensorFlow, Spark MLlib, Scikit-learn) carry a $20–25K salary premium over core skills like SQL and Python.
  • Remote roles pay about $4,400 more than on-site roles but draw 59% more applicants.
  • Average pay more than doubles from entry level ($80K) to lead ($171K).

The postings are a synthetic dataset built with generate_dataset.py. The project demonstrates the pipeline and SQL, not real market figures.
